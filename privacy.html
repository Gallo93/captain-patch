Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

"Captain Patch Uploader" (the "App") is an internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Captain Patch. It uses the YouTube API Services only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel.

Data we access

The App requests only the youtube.upload permission for the channel owner's account. It does not access, collect, store or share personal data of any other user, viewer or third party.

Data we store

The App stores an OAuth refresh token for the owner's channel in Google Cloud Secret Manager, and a log of the videos it uploaded (title, date, video ID) in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket. No data is sold or shared.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

YouTube and Google

By using YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
